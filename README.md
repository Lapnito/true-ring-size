## What is True Ring Size?

**True Ring Size** is an accurate, fully offline ring sizer for iPhone and Android that turns your phone into a real ring gauge — no printing, no paper strips, no physical sizer to order and wait for. When you need to know a ring size right now, you open the app, measure on the screen, and read the size in every international system at once.

There are two ways to measure. With **ring-on-screen**, you place a ring you already own flat on the display and resize an on-screen circle until it matches the inside of the ring. With **finger-on-screen**, you line your finger up between the guides and read the size directly. Either way you calibrate once with any standard bank card (they are all the same ISO size), so the app knows exactly how many pixels equal one millimeter on your specific screen — that is what makes the reading trustworthy instead of a rough guess.

Once measured, True Ring Size converts instantly between **US, UK, EU (ISO), Japan, China and Russia** sizing systems, by **diameter or circumference**, in **millimeters or inches**. You can save sizes for the whole family with a note and a date — ideal for buying a surprise gift or an engagement ring without asking and giving it away.

Where almost every competing ring sizer hides saving, conversion or the whole app behind a subscription, an in-app purchase or a wall of ads, True Ring Size ships everything for free, works entirely offline, and collects no data at all. On Android it is even built **without the internet permission**, so it is verifiably impossible for the app to send anything anywhere.

## How True Ring Size Works

**1. Calibrate once.** Place any bank or ID card on the screen and resize the outline until it matches the card. Because every card is exactly the same ISO size, the app now knows precisely how many pixels equal one millimeter on your screen. You only do this once per device.

**2. Measure.** Put an existing ring on the screen and size the circle to its inside edge, or place your finger between the guides. The measurement is taken in real millimeters thanks to the calibration.

**3. Read and convert.** The size appears in US, UK, EU (ISO), Japan, China and Russia at once, by diameter or circumference, in mm or inches. Save it with a name if it is for someone else.

**Does it need an internet connection?** No. Every feature works in airplane mode. The Android build does not even request the internet permission.

**How accurate is it?** Accuracy depends on your screen and careful calibration. Calibrate with a card and measure twice, and the reading is reliable enough to order a ring online with confidence. For a permanent or high-value purchase, it is always wise to confirm with a jeweler.

## FAQ

**Is True Ring Size really free?** Yes. No ads, no subscription, no in-app purchases, no account. Every feature — both measuring modes, all conversions and saving sizes — is free.

**How do I measure a ring size without a physical sizer?** Two ways. Put a ring you already own on the screen and resize the on-screen circle to match its inside edge, or place your finger between the on-screen guides. Calibrate once with a bank card first for accurate, real-millimeter results.

**Why do I need to calibrate with a card?** Every phone screen has a different pixel density, so the app can't know real-world sizes on its own. A bank or ID card is a fixed ISO size worldwide, so matching the outline to a card teaches the app the exact pixels-per-millimeter of your screen. It takes a few seconds and you only do it once.

**How accurate is measuring on a screen?** With careful calibration and by measuring twice, it is accurate enough to order a ring online with confidence. Results depend on your screen, so for an expensive or permanent purchase it is wise to double-check with a jeweler.

**Which sizing systems does it convert between?** US, UK, EU (ISO), Japan, China and Russia — by inner diameter or circumference, in millimeters or inches. There is also a full conversion chart you can browse any time.

**Can I save sizes for other people?** Yes. Save a size per person with a name, finger/hand and note — ideal for surprise gifts, engagement rings and wedding bands. Only the raw measurement is stored, so every system stays correct if the conversion tables are ever updated.

**Does it work offline?** Completely. Every feature works with no internet connection, even in airplane mode. The Android version is built without the internet permission at all.

**Does it collect my data?** No. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing you measure leaves your device. The store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

**Is it good for buying an engagement ring secretly?** Yes — that is one of its main uses. Measure one of your partner's existing rings on the screen, save the size with a note, and order the right size without asking.

**Do I need to print anything?** No. Unlike printable paper ring sizers, everything happens on the phone screen — nothing to print, cut out or wrap around a finger.

**Is it available on Android and iPhone?** Yes. True Ring Size is on both the App Store (iPhone) and Google Play (Android).
